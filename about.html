Our Story

Built around the craft of grooming.

The Fade Room was created for people who appreciate a great cut, a clean environment and the confidence that comes from looking their best.

More Than A Haircut

A modern barbershop with old-school attention to detail.

The Fade Room started with a simple idea: create a barber shop where excellent craftsmanship and a modern experience belong in the same chair.

Our approach combines traditional barbering discipline with contemporary cuts, fades and grooming techniques. We take the time to understand each client's style rather than treating every appointment the same.

Whether you're preparing for an important meeting, refreshing your everyday look or simply taking some time for yourself, our goal is to make every visit feel worthwhile.
